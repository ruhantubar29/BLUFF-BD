🎲 About BLUFFᴮᴰ

BLUFFᴮᴰ is a collection of fun party games designed to bring people together.

Whether you're playing alone, with friends, or passing the phone around the room, BLUFFᴮᴰ brings multiple games together in one place.

The goal is simple:

«Open the app. Pick a game. Start playing.»

No complicated setup. Just games, friends, and chaos. 😈

---

🎮 Game Modes

👤 Singleplayer

Play by yourself or challenge AI/bots where supported.

🌐 Multiplayer

Play online with friends and other players.

📱 Pass & Play

One device. Multiple players.

Pass the phone around and let everyone take their turn.
